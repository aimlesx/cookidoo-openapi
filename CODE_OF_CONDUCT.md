# Code of conduct

We are committed to a welcoming, harassment-free project for everyone,
regardless of background, identity, experience, or viewpoint.

Be respectful, discuss evidence rather than people, accept correction, and
avoid language or behavior that a reasonable person would consider hostile,
demeaning, threatening, or invasive. Do not pressure contributors to reveal
accounts, credentials, identity, or private research material.

Maintainers may edit or remove comments, commits, issues, and other
contributions that violate this standard, and may temporarily or permanently
restrict participation. Enforcement decisions should be proportionate,
documented privately, and protect reporters' privacy.

Report conduct concerns privately to the repository owner through the private
contact method on their GitHub profile. Do not include credentials, service
vulnerabilities, or personal data in a conduct report.
