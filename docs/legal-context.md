## EU Data Act

EU users may have separate statutory rights concerning access to certain data
generated by connected products and related services under
[Regulation (EU) 2023/2854](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/legal-content/EN/TXT/?uri=CELEX%3A32023R2854).
Vorwerk publishes Cookidoo-specific information for Poland and identifies
`EUDataAct@vorwerk.com` as a data-access contact. This project is independent
of that official process. The EU Data Act does not by itself make this observed
interface public or grant rights to reproduce Cookidoo recipe content.

Relevant background also includes the
[Software Directive 2009/24/EC](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/legal-content/EN/ALL/?uri=celex%3A32009L0024),
the [Polish Copyright Act](https://eli.gov.pl/eli/DU/2025/24/ogl), and the
[Database Directive 96/9/EC](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/legal-content/EN/ALL/?uri=celex%3A31996L0009).
Their application is fact-specific; none is presented here as blanket
permission to access or publish.
